WEEK 15 SUMMARY — Jiang et al. (2026) Replication: American (OEX) IV Forecasting

================================================================
MOTIVATION
================================================================
Replicate Jiang, Lazar, Marra (2026, Journal of Futures Markets 46, 1137-1153)
on the Week 14 S&P 100 American (OEX) options dataset. The goal is to forecast
market-implied volatility using four neural network frameworks across three
forecast horizons, reproducing the paper's Tables 4 (in-sample), 5 (out-of-sample),
and 6 (call/put x moneyness breakdown).

================================================================
SHARED INFRASTRUCTURE (jiang_common.py)
================================================================
- JiangNN: 32->16->8->1 MLP, ReLU, Xavier Normal init, 769 params (Table 3).
  in_dim=2 (moneyness, tau) for BS+NN/Heston+NN/Pure NN; in_dim=3 reserved
  for the Week 16 HestonFeature extension.
- Black-Scholes (Merton 1973 with continuous dividend yield q), Brent inversion,
  Carr-Wu (2010) de-Americanization.
- Heston via Albrecher et al. (2007) phi_2 characteristic function, priced with
  vectorized Carr-Madan (1999) FFT (N=4096, eta=0.25, alpha=1.5).
- RMSE reported as RMSE * 100 in percentage points of IV (matches Tables 4-6).
- Platform: macOS M4, spawn/fork multiprocessing. Epochs reduced 800->200 and
  batch 32->64 for ~4x speedup with negligible accuracy loss.

================================================================
PIPELINE
================================================================
1. De-Americanize OptionMetrics American IVs into European-equivalent prices
   (vectorized BS, intrinsic-value floors).
2. Daily BS calibration (single sigma_BS per day, eq 5) and Heston calibration
   (separate call/put parameter sets, eq 6) in parallel across trading days
   with warm starts from the prior day and checkpoint-every-20-days resume.
3. Extract sigma_Heston per option via vectorized bisection inversion of FFT
   model prices; build three label columns:
     label_bs_nn     = sigma_market - sigma_bs
     label_heston_nn = sigma_market - sigma_heston
     label_pure_nn   = sigma_market

================================================================
FRAMEWORKS TRAINED (run_framework)
================================================================
- BS+NN Independent and Sequential
- Heston+NN Independent and Sequential
- Pure NN Sequential (same-day and one-day-ahead)
Training modes: independent (fresh model each day) vs sequential (one model
carried forward). Horizons: same-day (70/30 R-split), one-day-ahead, five-day-ahead.
In-sample RMSE (Table 4) and out-of-sample RMSE (Table 5) recorded per day,
plus ITM/ATM/OTM bucket RMSEs.

================================================================
NOTES
================================================================
Observation count differs from Jiang's 2,572,366 due to OptionMetrics
retroactive database updates; mean and std IV match Tables 1-2, confirming
correct sourcing and filtering. Per Jiang Section 4.5, Pure NN Independent is
omitted (equivalent to BS+NN Independent). In-sample RMSE for 1-day and 5-day
horizons is identical (both train on day t, paper footnote 2).

================================================================
ARTIFACTS
================================================================
- amer_with_labels.csv (de-Americanized prices + sigma_bs/sigma_heston + labels)
- calib_checkpoint.pkl (sigma_bs_map, heston_map)
- results_{framework}_{mode}_{horizon}.json (daily IS/OOS RMSE + bucket RMSEs)
