WEEK 17 SUMMARY — Comprehensive Framework Comparison: 18 Configurations

================================================================
OBJECTIVE
================================================================
Aggregate the full battery of frameworks across all three forecast horizons and
build comparison tables and visualizations to observe relationships and draw
conclusions. Each "framework" is one (model x training x horizon) configuration
carrying both an in-sample RMSE (Table 4 style) and an out-of-sample RMSE
(Table 5 style).

The 18 frameworks (6 per horizon):
  BS+NN Indep, BS+NN Seq, Heston+NN Indep, Heston+NN Seq, Pure NN Seq,
  HestonFeature Seq (extension)
  x {same-day, one-day-ahead, five-day-ahead}

================================================================
COMPARISON TABLES (vs Jiang et al.)
================================================================
Table A — In-sample (Table 4) + out-of-sample (Table 5), AM to AM, R-splitting.
  Our IS/OOS RMSE beside paper values with absolute and percent differences.
  5-day in-sample equals 1-day in-sample (shared Panel C). HestonFeature has no
  paper counterpart (shown as —).
Table B — OOS RMSE by Call/Put x ITM/ATM/OTM (Table 6 Panel B), sequential models.
  Paper values rendered beneath ours, green where we beat the paper, red where not.
Table 6 (all frameworks) — full 6-cell Call/Put x bucket layout across all three
  horizons, paper values shown only where Jiang reports them, with a per-framework
  Avg column.

================================================================
ANALYSIS (where error concentrates, who wins)
================================================================
Four questions answered from the table6 JSONs (sample-averaged OOS RMSE %):
  (1) Hardest moneyness bucket — ITM/ATM/OTM ranking; ATM easiest, tails hardest.
  (2) Calls vs Puts — which is predicted better on average.
  (3) Lowest-overall framework — ranked across all buckets and horizons, plus a
      same-day-only ranking for a cleaner apples-to-apples read.
  (4) Whether HestonFeature wins — count of cells where it has the lowest RMSE
      and its overall rank among all frameworks.
A framework x moneyness heatmap (greener = lower error) summarizes the picture.

================================================================
TIME-SERIES VISUALIZATIONS (60-day MA OOS RMSE)
================================================================
Stacked six-panel figures, one per focal horizon (same-day / 1-day / 5-day),
each with the focal horizon drawn bright and the other two faint for context:
  Panel 1 — Independent vs Sequential (BS+NN, Heston+NN)
  Panel 2 — By framework (sequential)
  Panel 3 — Bucket RMSE ITM/ATM/OTM (HestonFeature solid, Pure NN dashed)
  Panel 4a/4b — Call/Put x bucket for HestonFeature and Pure NN
All curves use a strict 60-trading-day window, matching Jiang Figs 3-6.
